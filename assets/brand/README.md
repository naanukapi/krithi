# Krithi Kailar identity

The selected generated assets are `krithi-kailar-wordmark.png` (title typography) and `kk-monogram.png` (compact icon). They were produced with built-in image generation. The full-size originals are retained here for reuse.

Direction: restrained high-contrast Roman serif typography, sculptural K arms inspired by Bharatanatyam, subtle curved terminals and bindu-like dots. No dancer illustration, temple frame or ornamental border. Black artwork on white, composited by the website as light lettering over its dark backgrounds.

The wordmark brief requested the exact words “Krithi Kailar”, on two centered, closely spaced lines, with flat black ink, crisp contours, no glow, shadow, gradient or mockup. The icon brief requested two interlocking capital K letters in one compact, balanced monogram, a subtle sweeping lower leg and a single bindu dot, legible at favicon size.
