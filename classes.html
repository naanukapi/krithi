Akashabharati is the academy founded by Krithi Kailar, through which she teaches students across the country. The new offline classes bring that continuing practice to Jayanagara, Mysuru.

Krithi’s own training spans the Pandanallur and Kalakshetra bānis. Her study of music and the Nāṭyaśāstra informs the relationship between rhythm, movement and meaning.
